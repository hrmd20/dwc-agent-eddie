# Google topic images and rounded picture cards

Use this treatment when MASTER EDITOR's image mode is selected. It does not add pictures to a captions-only brief. Explicit user choices override these defaults.

## Choose the picture

1. Use the user's supplied or selected image for that cue first. Otherwise search Google Images for the actual spoken subject, adding context and `landscape` or `16:9` when helpful. Prefer a relevant landscape photograph near 16:9 over a square graphic, portrait crop, or unrelated product bottle. The video itself remains portrait 9:16 unless requested otherwise.
2. Pick the first suitable result that communicates the concept clearly at phone size, has usable resolution, and leaves the essential subject/labels intact. Do not pick an irrelevant first result just because it appears first. For a symptom such as erectile dysfunction, a non-explicit photograph of a concerned adult or couple may communicate the topic better than anatomy; use an anatomical image when the spoken point is anatomy. Product/drug mentions can use the actual package or vial; ingredients should show the ingredient unless a product is requested.
3. Open the result and inspect the actual image. Keep the Google query, source-page URL, image URL and local asset in the private project. If Google is unavailable, report that and identify any substitute search service. Do not silently substitute generated art.
4. Fit supplied images proportionally even if they are not 16:9. Do not stretch them or crop off clinical labels, key subjects or watermarks to force a ratio. Prefer a better landscape search result when a sourced asset does not fit. Transparent vial cutouts and useful anatomical/conversion diagrams remain valid exceptions to the photographic-card preference.

## Style the overlay

- Keep the presenter as the base; place the picture directly below stationary captions with a visible gap and clear of the face. Show it at the matching spoken phrase and remove it when that topic ends. Preserve approved cue timing during styling-only revisions.
- Rectangular photo, product-photo and diagram cards default to rounded corners and a subtle soft drop shadow. A useful 2160-wide starting point is maximum picture bounds 1700×1080, corner radius 52 px, black shadow at about 45% opacity, blur 20 px and downward offset 16 px. Scale these values proportionally; inspect against the actual frame rather than treating them as mandatory coordinates.
- Reserve transparent padding around the shadow (about 64 px at 2160-wide) so the blur is not clipped. Keep the visible picture's position fixed after adding padding. Preserve full content inside the card; rounding must not obscure important labels.
- Keep requested transparent vial cutouts transparent; do not force them onto a rectangular white card. A dark line diagram needs a contrasting backing so its labels remain readable.
- Pictures remain still with the standard instant entry/exit. Rounded corners and shadows do not authorize extra motion, music or sound effects.

## Check the encoded result

Inspect every distinct image at phone size, along with its entry and exit. Confirm correct subject, proportions, visible rounding/shadow, readable labels, clear captions/face and topic-aligned timing. Supplied images stay private; only reusable instructions belong in Git.
