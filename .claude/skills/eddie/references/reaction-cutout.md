# Doctor cutout and source-video overlay

Use for reaction edits that need a foreground doctor cutout. Preserve an approved edit or an explicitly requested layout. The [Instagram reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dff0wi5jdCb/) is the requested reference. Apply the described layout and calibrate the exact placement from the footage.

## Build the shot

1. Reframe the doctor recording to the vertical canvas and remove the background from the foreground copy with a tracked person matte or a clean chroma key. Preserve hair, hands and any microphone needed in frame. Check edges throughout movement and refine unstable masks before delivery.
2. Keep the reframed doctor shot as the base. Place the original moving source clip in a separate overlay above the base, then put the masked doctor foreground above the source overlay as needed. Choose placement per shot. For Sync, use rounded corners and a soft shadow per the [Sync Reaction recipe](reaction-sync.md). Preserve the source aspect ratio and embedded captions. Resize or reposition the overlay and doctor so the important source action and text stay visible.
3. Place the [approved EDDIE hook](text-hook.md) in the top safe area, above the source overlay when space permits. Use one snug translucent charcoal box with bold white text and a yellow keyword. Keep the hook and captions on independent canvas layers after reframing. Check the opening, source cuts and any zooms for overlap or drift.
4. Follow the chosen reaction timing. For Sync, align the doctor and source during playback. Use the original source audio alone until the overlay ends, then start the doctor’s recorded audio. For Async, play the source excerpt before the doctor's response. Use a matching silent reaction plate or an approved still for the cutout during the excerpt when available. If there is no usable plate, use a full-screen source opening and cut directly to the doctor's response. Use the existing Sync or Async hook duration unless the brief specifies another duration.
5. At the end of the source excerpt, remove the overlay and matte together for full-screen doctor commentary. Preserve embedded source captions, use separate doctor captions, and avoid doubled playback audio. Apply the shared third polishing pass and verify the cutout, hook, captions and handoff on the encoded 4K review export.
