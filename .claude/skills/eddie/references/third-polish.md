# Mandatory third polishing layer — every video

This is the final release layer after (1) speech assembly and (2) captions, visuals and audio. Existing detailed speech-audit passes still apply inside those layers. Internal QC renders are necessary; do not deliver a video, place it in DONE, or upload it until the checklist passes. If review is unavailable, report the blocker without delivering an unchecked export unless the user explicitly requests a provisional preview.

## Checklist — record evidence against the exact export SHA-256

- **Loudness:** balance speakers, apply conservative isolation/denoise, then measure and normalize the complete mix to the user's −14 LUFS integrated target. This replaces the historical automatic 150% boost. Use measured two-pass normalization, target encoded true peak at or below −1 dBTP, and verify the actual AAC export. Accept −14 ±0.5 LUFS; missing/non-finite measurements fail. Avoid pumping, clipped consonants and flattened dynamics; listen at matched loudness. Do not stack gain on processed exports. Record input/output integrated loudness, true peak and processing settings. A microphone next batch does not waive measurement.
- **Text during zooms:** transform only the footage layer. Composite captions and hooks afterward in fixed output-canvas coordinates; keep the same baseline, font size and box geometry across base shots and punch-ins. Do not attach text to face tracking, a scaled wrapper, or per-shot crop coordinates. Inspect before/during/after EVERY zoom/crop transition in the encoded file. Save timestamped evidence of stable anchors; no downward drift is acceptable. Preserve explicitly requested text animation only where authorized.
- **Clean cuts:** listen to every join with at least 1.5 seconds of context on both sides at normal speed. Repair clicks, abrupt room-tone changes, clipped syllables and unnatural gaps from source audio. Use short audio fades only in non-speech handles, never to hide missing consonants. Recheck repaired joins.
- **Complete endings:** compare the final 10 seconds with the source and approved closing thought. Preserve the full last word, final consonant and natural decay, typically 250–500 ms after speech. Never truncate a sentence to hit a duration target; shorten elsewhere. Confirm audio and video end together naturally.
- **Repeated sentences:** audit the whole recording and export, not only flagged timestamps. Compare full transcription with overlapping short excerpts and listen for repeated sentences, restarted clauses, repeated words and false starts. ASR can omit repetitions. Keep the cleanest complete take; retain intentional emphasis only with a recorded reason. Preserve clinical qualifiers and meaning.
- **Whole-file playback:** watch AND listen from beginning to end at normal speed. Check sync, captions, hook duration, visual timing and speech continuity. Record reviewer, timestamps and evidence; automated scans do not substitute for listening.
- **Release:** run EDDIE Quality Check on this exact encoded file. All required checks Passed, zero open findings, and all checklist items evidenced are required. Any edit invalidates prior file-bound approval; rerun checks and playback on the new export. Never claim zero errors solely from a successful render.
