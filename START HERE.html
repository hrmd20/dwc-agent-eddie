What happens when I download this?

This is a guide and skill package—not a standalone video editor. It does not install software, start a chat or connect accounts automatically. Your assistant checks the editing tools and helps with any one-time setup. Your assistant and optional services may have their own costs.

Only have a script? EDDIE can plan the edit first. For Rapid Fire, questions can be recorded by your nurse or generated with an AI voice when you request it.
